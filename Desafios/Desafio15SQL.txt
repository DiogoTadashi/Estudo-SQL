DESAFIO INNER JOIN
Trazer um resultado que inclua: BusinessEntityID, Name, PhoneNumberTypeID e PhoneNumber (utilizando joins entre tabelas).

//RESOLUÇÂO
SELECT pp.BusinessEntityID, pt.Name, pp.PhoneNumberTypeID ,pp.PhoneNumber
FROM person.PhoneNumberType as pt
JOIN person.PersonPhone AS pp ON pp.PhoneNumberTypeID = pt.PhoneNumberTypeID



Trazer um resultado que inclua: AddressID, Name, StateProvinceID e City (utilizando joins entre tabelas).

//RESOLUÇÂO
SELECT ad.AddressID, ad.City, sp.StateProvinceID, sp.Name
FROM person.StateProvince as sp
JOIN person.Address AS ad ON sp.StateProvinceID = ad.StateProvinceID
